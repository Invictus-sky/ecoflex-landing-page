The World Is Changing. Your Cup Should Too.

Lagos generates between 13,000 and 15,000 metric tonnes of waste daily, with a significant portion coming from single-use plastics materials that take up to 500 years to degrade. The styrofoam takeaway containers you see everywhere clogging drainages, floating in the lagoon, burning by roadsides are now officially banned. The Lagos State Government began by banning styrofoam food packs in 2024 and announced a complete phase-out of all single-use plastics within 12 months, with full enforcement commencing July 1, 2025. Any company that fails to adhere to the ban faces prosecution and being sealed off. This isn't a trend. It's a policy with teeth. And the question now isn't whether to change it's what you'll change to. That's where Ecoflex comes in. Our collapsible silicone cups are reusable, food-safe, pocket-sized, and built for everyday Nigerian life. One cup. Endless refills. Zero plastic guilt. The future is reusable.
